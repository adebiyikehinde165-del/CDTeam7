1.) PRODUCT OVERVIEW

&#x20;   The Campus Shuttle/ Transit Wait and Capacity Reporting System is a proposed platform designed to help students and staff access reliable information about campus transportation. it aims to address challenges such as long waiting times, shuttle unavailability and overcrowded vehicles.

&#x20;   The system will allow users to view updates on shuttle availability, vehicle capacity and crowd levels at different stops. Its main goal is to reduce uncertainty, improve access to transportation information and make movement around campus easier and more convenient.



2.) PRODUCT STATEMENT

&#x20;   Students and staff who depend on campus shuttles often experience difficulties knowing when a shuttle is available, how long they may have to wait, or whether a vehicle has enough space. During busy periods, this can lead to long waiting times, overcrowding and delays in getting to their destinations.

&#x20;   The lack of a reliable way to access updated shuttle information makes transportation less convenient and creates uncertainty for passengers.

3.) BACKGROUND AND CONTEXT

&#x20;   Campus transportation is very important and plays the role of helping both students and staffs to make their way around the campus. &#x20;   campus transportation updates.

7.) EXISTING SOLUTIONS AND THEIR LIMITATIONS



Students currently rely on different methods to get information about campus shuttle availability. These include asking shuttle drivers, making enquiries from other students, using WhatsApp group chats and waiting at shuttle stops.

&#x20;     However, these methods have some limitations. Information shared through conversations or group chats may not always be accurate or up to date. Asking drivers or other students may also provide limited information, while waiting at shuttle stops without knowing when a vehicle will arrive can lead to unnecessary delays.



8.) PROPOSED SOLUTION



The proposed solution is a Campus Shuttle / Transit Wait and Capacity Reporting System that will provide a centralised platform for accessing campus transportation updates. The software will allow authorised users to submit information about shuttle availability, vehicle capacity and crowd levels at different stops. Students and staff will be able to see these updates to make better transportation decisions and reduce their waiting time.

The platform will also display when information was last updated to help users identify recent and outdated reports.

11.) FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

The system should be able to perform the following functions:

\- Allow users to create accounts and log in.

\- Allow authorised contributors to submit shuttle availability updates.

\- Allow contributors to report vehicle capacity and crowd levels.

\- Store shuttle, stop and route information.

\- Record the date and time of every submitted report.

\- Validate submitted information before storing it.

\- Allow users to view the latest available shuttle updates.

\- Identify outdated reports and prevent them from being displayed as current information.

\- Allow administrators to manage users and submitted reports.

14.) PROPOSED SYSTEM WORKFLOW

&#x20;  The system will follow these steps:

&#x20;- An authorised contributor logs into the platform.

&#x20;- The contributor submits an update about shuttle availability or capacity.

&#x20;- The backend checks and validates the submitted information.

&#x20;- The report is stored with its date and time.

&#x20;- student or staff member requests shuttle information.

&#x20;- The backend retrieves the latest available report.

&#x20;- The system displays the information to the user, including when it was last updated.



15.) BACKEND AND DATA REQUIREMENTS

&#x20;  The backend will be responsible for storing, managing and retrieving the information required by the system.

&#x20;  The main data entities will include:

&#x20;- Users: Stores account details of people using the platform.

&#x20;- Roles: Defines the permissions assigned to different users.

&#x20;- Shuttles: Stores information about available campus vehicles.

&#x20;- Stops: Contains details of designated shuttle stops.

&#x20;- Routes: Stores information about shuttle routes.

&#x20;- Reports: Stores submitted updates about shuttle availability, capacity and crowd levels.

&#x20;TIP: Each report will include relevant information such as the submission time, contributor and the shuttle or stop being reported.



16.) REPORT VALIDATION AND FRESHNESS



The system will check submitted reports to ensure that the information is complete and provided by authorised users. Each report will have a timestamp showing when it was submitted.



The backend will use these timestamps to identify recent and outdated information. This will help prevent users from relying on old reports and ensure that the platform displays the most relevant available updates.



The system will also include measures to reduce false, incomplete and repeated submissions.



17.) CONSTRAINTS AND RISKS



The development and use of the system may face certain challenges, including:

&#x20;- Poor Internet Connectivity: Users may experience difficulties accessing or submitting updates.

&#x20;- Inaccurate Reports: Some contributors may provide incorrect shuttle information.

&#x20;- Limited Participation: Not enough users may be available to submit regular updates.

&#x20;- Conflicting Information: Different contributors may submit different reports about the same shuttle.

&#x20;- Limited Resources: Time, funding and technical resources may affect development.
